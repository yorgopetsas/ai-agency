## Section 4: Beyond the Basics — Advanced Concepts (The Deep Dive)

**(This section introduces the "how" behind the magic, keeping it fascinating.)**

### The Secret Sauce: Mixture-of-Experts (MoE)
The 26B and larger models use something called **Mixture-of-Experts (MoE)**. Think of this as a team of specialists working together!
*   **How it works:** Instead of every part of the network checking every piece of data for every question, the model intelligently decides which "expert" (which part of the training data or skill set) is best suited for the task at hand.
*   **Relatable Analogy:** It’s like having a team of specialized consultants for a big project. One expert handles the math, another handles the creative writing, and a third handles the coding—all collaborating seamlessly. This is why the 26B model is so efficient!
